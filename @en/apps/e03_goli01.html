GoL∞ is an app for observing Conway’s Game of Life on an infinite grid. Live cells change from generation to generation by the standard Conway rules.
In addition, the app’s unique system replicates eligible cell groups into open regions.
Live cells that remain unchanged in the same position and become stationary also die after a certain number of generations.
You can switch between the normal view and a view that uses nonlinear radial compression to fit the full range of live cells into a circle.
Switching views does not change the simulation rules.
